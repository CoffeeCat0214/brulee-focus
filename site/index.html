CoffeeCat

A cozy pixel coffee cat for your browser.

Keep a tiny companion on the pages you browse, start a gentle Focus Coffee timer, and watch the cup drain as time runs out.

(02)

V2 Gentle Gatekeeper

Coffee's gone. Tiny break.

CoffeeCat V2 turns the end of a Slow Pour into a humane interruption. Wherever you are browsing, the page fills with semi-transparent coffee, CoffeeCat steps in, and a five-minute break begins. Refill when you are ready, or snooze once.

The page stays visible underneath. Nothing is blocked, closed, or sent anywhere.

(03)

Browser buddy

A desk pet that lives on the page.

CoffeeCat sits on normal web pages rather than the browser frame, so it is always in the same place you are already looking.

01

Drag anywhere

Move the cat wherever it suits the page. The position is remembered across tabs and sessions, and one click resets it to the corner.

02

Three sizes

Small, medium, or large — 64, 88, or 116 pixels — depending on whether you want a companion or a barely-there presence.

03

Click to sip and purr

Clicking the cat plays a short sip animation and a soft synthesized purr. No audio files, no autoplay, nothing plays until you click.

(04)

Privacy

Local by default.

CoffeeCat has no backend, no analytics, no accounts, and no social login. There is nowhere for your data to go.

01

What it stores

Whether the cat is shown, its size, its position on the page, the current timer state, and local focus stats — sessions completed, minutes protected, cups finished. All of it in Chrome extension storage.

02

What it never touches

CoffeeCat does not collect browsing history or page content, and requests exactly one permission: storage.

(05)

Install

Load it locally.

CoffeeCat is not published to the Chrome Web Store yet. For now, load the unpacked folder.

  1. Open chrome://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked.
  4. Select the CoffeeCat folder.
  5. Visit a normal web page and start Focus Coffee.
(06)

Technical notes

CoffeeCat is a Manifest V3 extension. It requests the storage permission only, and runs on http and https pages.

Chrome does not allow extensions to draw freely in the native tab strip, so CoffeeCat appears as an on-page buddy instead of a character that walks along your tabs.

The coffee flood is a local, page-level break reminder. It is not a network blocker and does not prevent you from browsing.
