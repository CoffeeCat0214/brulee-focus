CoffeeCat

A very small cat who takes coffee seriously.

Crème Brûlée keeps to the corner of whatever page you happen to be reading. Pour a Focus Coffee and her cup begins to empty, quietly, in time with your afternoon.

(02)

Coffee break

The cup's empty. Time to stop.

When a Slow Pour ends, coffee rises over the page and Crème Brûlée sits with you for five minutes. Refill when you're ready, or snooze once.

Your page stays visible underneath. Nothing is blocked, closed, or sent anywhere.

(03)

In residence

Meet Crème Brûlée.

She sits on ordinary web pages rather than in the browser frame, which is to say, exactly where you are already looking.

01

She goes where you put her

Move her anywhere the page has room. She remembers the spot across tabs and sessions, and returns to her corner with a single click.

02

Three sizes

Small, medium, or large: 64, 88, or 116 pixels. A proper companion, or the faintest suggestion of one.

03

A click, a sip, a purr

Click her and she takes a sip and purrs, the whole thing synthesized on the spot. No audio files, no autoplay. She is silent until invited.

(04)

Privacy

Discreet by nature.

No backend, no analytics, no accounts. There is nowhere for anything to go.

01

What she keeps

Whether she is visible, her size and her spot on the page, the state of the timer, and your own quiet tally: sessions finished, minutes protected, cups emptied. All of it in Chrome's extension storage.

02

What she never touches

Your browsing history and the contents of your pages. She reads neither, and asks for a single permission: storage.

(05)

Install

Bringing her home.

She is not on the Chrome Web Store yet. Until she is: five steps and an unpacked folder.

  1. Open chrome://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked.
  4. Select the CoffeeCat folder.
  5. Open any web page and pour the first coffee.
(06)

Technical notes

CoffeeCat is a Manifest V3 extension. It requests the storage permission only, and runs on http and https pages.

Chrome does not allow extensions to draw freely in the native tab strip, which is why she lives on the page rather than padding along your tabs.

The coffee break is a local, page-level reminder to rest. It is not a site blocker, and it never stops you browsing.
