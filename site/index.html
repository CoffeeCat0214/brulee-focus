V2 Gentle Gatekeeper

Coffee's gone. Tiny break.

CoffeeCat V2 turns the end of a focus session into a shareable, humane interruption. On domains you choose, CoffeeCat steps into the page, starts a five-minute break, and gives you one snooze.

CoffeeCat protected 25 focus minutes 1 cup finished locally

Focus Coffee

The cup mirrors your timer and drains smoothly several times per second, so time passing is visible without needing a loud alarm.

Your domains

Add distracting sites yourself. CoffeeCat stays gentle and predictable because the break overlay appears only where you asked for protection.

Local by default

No backend, analytics, account, or social login. Settings and focus stats stay in Chrome extension storage.

Install locally

  1. Open chrome://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked.
  4. Select the CoffeeCat folder.
  5. Visit a normal web page and start Focus Coffee.
