Brûlée Focus

Meet Brûlée, your Coffee Cat.

A cozy focus timer that helps you work and rest. Brûlée keeps to the corner of whatever page you happen to be reading, and her cup empties quietly, in time with your afternoon.

(02)

Intermission

The cup's empty. Time to stop.

When any brew ends, coffee rises over the page and Crème Brûlée sits with you for five minutes. Refill when you're ready, or snooze and carry on without restarting.

Your page stays visible underneath. Nothing is blocked, closed, or sent anywhere.

(03)

In residence

Meet Crème Brûlée.

She sits on ordinary web pages rather than in the browser frame, which is to say, exactly where you are already looking.

01

She goes where you put her

Move her anywhere the page has room. She remembers the spot across tabs and sessions, and returns to her corner with a single click.

02

Three sizes

Small, medium, or large: 64, 88, or 116 pixels. A proper companion, or the faintest suggestion of one.

03

A click, a sip, a purr

Click her and she takes a sip and purrs, the whole thing synthesized on the spot. No audio files, no autoplay. She is silent until invited.

(04)

Privacy

Discreet by nature.

No backend, no analytics, no accounts. There is nowhere for anything to go.

01

What she keeps

Whether she is visible, her size and her spot on the page, the state of the timer, and your own quiet tally: sessions finished, minutes protected, cups emptied. All of it in local extension storage, on your machine, not synced to your account.

02

What she never touches

Your browsing history and the contents of your pages. She reads neither, and asks for two permissions: storage to remember the above, and alarms so a session can end on time when no page is open.

03

In full

The privacy policy says the same thing at greater length, including why Chrome still shows a broad-sounding permission warning for an extension that sends nothing anywhere.

(05)

Technical notes

Brûlée Focus is a Manifest V3 extension. It requests the storage permission and runs on http and https pages so Crème Brûlée can sit beside what you are reading.

Chrome does not allow extensions to draw freely in the native tab strip, which is why she lives on the page rather than padding along your tabs.

The coffee break is a local, page-level reminder to rest. It is not a site blocker, and it never stops you browsing.

(06)

Install

Bringing her home.

She is on the Chrome Web Store, and she asks for two permissions on the way in. Four steps, and she is on every page you open.

  1. Open the Brûlée Focus listing on the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Choose Add to Chrome.
  3. Approve the two she asks for: storage to remember your settings, alarms to end a session on time.
  4. Open any web page and pour the first coffee.

Prefer to run her from source? Turn on Developer mode at chrome://extensions, choose Load unpacked, and select the Brûlée Focus folder.
